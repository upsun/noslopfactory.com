# Product photography

These studio mockups were generated with the built-in imagegen tool on 27 October 2026. They are illustrations for a simulated shop, not photographs of manufactured products. WebP delivery images are stored in `public/products/`; the original PNG generator output remains in Codex's generated-images directory.

## Art direction

Square, photorealistic editorial studio product photographs. Pale warm grey seamless paper background, soft directional light from the upper left, gentle natural shadows, realistic tactile materials, and generous breathing room. Neutral monochrome palette. No people, hands, props, logos, extra lettering, borders, or watermarks.

Typography is a visual interpretation of an editorial serif for the assistant references and readable monospace for technical messages. The mockups do not embed a proprietary Claude font.

### Shirts

Use case: product-mockup. A single premium heavyweight cotton crewneck T-shirt, carefully styled overhead flat lay, fully visible and centered. Subtle folds and cotton texture. Garment occupies approximately 75 percent of the square. Print only the specified text, centered on the chest or upper back, in a large readable elegant editorial serif reminiscent of Claude's literary chat typography. Use warm off-white ink on washed black and near-black ink on off-white. For the long loading verb, explicitly spell F-l-i-b-b-e-r-t-i-g-i-b-b-e-t-i-n-g and require all letters.

### Caps

Use case: product-mockup. A single premium unstructured six-panel cotton baseball cap, front three-quarter view, brim toward the viewer and slightly left. Entire cap visible, occupying approximately 65 percent of the square. The only front-panel embroidery is the specified text in the specified serif or monospace style. Contrasting off-white or near-black thread with realistic restrained stitch texture.

### Stickers

Use case: product-mockup. A single premium matte off-white vinyl die-cut sticker, lying flat and slightly rotated on paper. Clean wide rounded rectangle, thin charcoal outline, white margin, visible paper thickness, delicate contact shadow. Top-down photograph with slight dimensionality. Large precise near-black monospace text, the specified smaller secondary line when present, and the specified small status symbol. Fully visible, occupying approximately 65 percent of square width.

## Asset handling

The original 1254 × 1254 PNG masters remain in `generated_images/01a17e19-4a67-7f52-90b2-2f37716b1779/` relative to the Codex home directory. The table below maps delivery names to source filenames. They are local generation artifacts, not repository dependencies.

Delivery images use WebP quality 86 at 1000 × 1000. All 13 delivery images together occupy 795,216 bytes; individual files range from 36,410 to 97,788 bytes. Product text is also stored as content so catalog names and accessible descriptions do not depend on reading the text within the generated image. Changing a content slogan does not change the corresponding photograph; regenerate that asset when artwork changes.
